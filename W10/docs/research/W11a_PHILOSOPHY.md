# W11a — the design philosophy, before any code

Written 2026-09-01, after four research strands and an audit of W10 against W8's own check
registry. The instruction was to settle the logic first rather than build and patch, because
W10's history is a pile of corrections and that is a fair criticism of how it was run.

One honest qualification before the principles. **Research alone would not have prevented
most of W10's defects.** The wadi rule never being called, the gradient stored for the wrong
diameter, the depth recorded after the reset instead of before, plots measured from
centroids — none of these was foreseeable by thinking harder. Every one surfaced only by
running something and measuring it. So this document is not a better plan. It is a set of
**invariants and an auditor** that catch what the plan misses, which is exactly what W8 had
and W10 threw away.

---

## The ten principles

### P1 — The auditor is written first, and the design is written to pass it

W8 had a registry of 22 named checks, each recomputing its constraint independently and
carrying its guideline page. W10 had none, and shipped with **2.80 km of surcharged trunk,
10.68 km over the d/D limit, 45.92 km below minimum cover, and 1.67 km of pipe running along
a dual carriageway**. Not one of those is subtle. They survived because nothing looked.

W11a starts by porting `W8/py/sewnet/stages/audit.py`, extending it to the **59-check
contract** in `W8_W10_POSTMORTEM.md` (50 blocking, 9 reporting), and running it against
W10's existing layers on day one. It fails immediately, and **that failing table is the
specification**.

### P2 — Every published number comes from exactly one function

Seven different lifting-station counts are in circulation from this project: 19, 21, 25, 37,
140, 184, 239. Each was computed ad hoc at the point of reporting. The station count moved
11 → 21 → 19 not because the design changed but because the definition did.

Rule: one function per published quantity, every document generated from it, and any metric
with a filter chain prints its own funnel (`N₀ → N₁ → N₂`) so a second filter cannot be
applied to an already-filtered set without it being visible.

### P3 — The output is the graph, not the geometry

`W10_pipes.shp` is **7,919 disconnected components at 0.01 m tolerance**, largest holding
5.9 %. The flow tree existed only in memory inside `p2_sizing.py` and was never written out.
91.4 % of the stitch links stop at exactly 1.000 m from what they join, because
`p0_auto.stitch()` buffers by 1.0 m before taking the nearest points — the 2.5 m snap in the
topology step masked it in the graph while the layer inherited the gaps.

Rule: every layer carries explicit `US_NODE` / `DS_NODE` identifiers written **from the
graph**. Connectivity is an attribute, never something to be re-derived by a tolerance. A
check asserts that reloading the published layers reproduces the graph exactly.

### P4 — A chamber is the unit of design, not a corridor segment

**4,763 of 20,936 reaches exceed the G203-p30 Table 12 spacing — 1,220 km, 64.8 % of the
length, longest reach 6,541 m.** W10 runs at 11.1 nodes/km against NAMA's built 32.3
manholes/km and W8's 19.8. Without chambers there is no schedule, no profile, no take-off,
no model and no package — one absence blocks five deliverables.

### P5 — The hierarchy is generated by a rule, not reproduced by a cap

W8 got the right shape by capping trunk joins and swept the cap to find it. Its own note
admits this was a proxy. The measured rule, from the properly labelled packages:

> **A lateral zone is one unbranched street run** (99.6 % a simple path; median 132 m,
> 5 chambers, 4 properties; cap 920 m). **At most 3 lateral zones and 750 m of flow path
> before a main.** **A sub main is a collector route defined by its outlet, not a load
> threshold.** **The trunk is traced from the outfall backwards.**

And the correction that matters: W8's *"91 %, median chain of 11"* is dominated by package
5A-1, which carries **zero TM and zero SM tokens across 1,123 pipes** — every zone parses as
a lateral because no other label exists there, and it is also the package with 0 % invert
levels. In the labelled packages the chain is **median 2, max 5**.

**The size of the gap: the as-built runs 14.8 % of its length as trunk plus sub main. W10's
main pipe is 4.5 % and it has no sub-main tier at all. W11a needs of the order of 270 km of
sub main that does not exist today.**

### P6 — Corridor provenance is carried to the end and never laundered

The four sources have genuinely different trust levels, and they were merged into one layer
with the differences lost:

| Source | km | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `draft` | 1,195.4 | **Good** — 0.05 % near a dual, 1.5 % through plots, 5.8 % on wadi |
| `auto_road` | 604.3 | **Usable, needs editing** — 11.0 % on wadi, and 50.8 % of the removable pipe on 27.7 % of the length |
| `auto_block` | 319.9 | **Provisional** — a *cadastral* street reserve, not an observed street. 45 % fronts plots of which **not one is built** |
| `auto_link` | 92.3 | **Poor** — 22.9 % through plots; every endpoint 1.0 m short |

The perverse result: **the sources trusted least were used most** — `auto_block` 97.4 %
converted to pipe and `auto_link` 99.9 %, against `draft` 76.3 %.

Rule: `SRC` and a `CONFIDENCE` grade travel on every pipe to the drawings and the schedules.
A corridor that is a cadastral reserve on bare desert is a legitimate design input for a
saturation horizon — but it must say so on its own record, and `auto_link` must be re-cut
so links actually touch.

### P7 — Scope is decided before the design, not discovered after it

**117.3 km (6.2 %) has no load-bearing plot within 60 m and carries under 1 m³/d.** It
neither collects nor conveys. **55 of 239 depth breaches and 832 m of the 3,083 m of lift —
27 % — sit on pipe that collects nothing.** Independently, 48 of 187 settlements cost 20 to
1,085 m of exclusive sewer per property against a network average of 13.8.

G201 §8 does not settle it: applied literally the size test catches 180 of 187 settlements
because the cadastre fragments, and the 25 km test catches one. The operative clause is
*"geographical barriers preventing economical connection"*, which hands the decision to the
25-year NPV at 5 % the project's financial method already runs.

Rule: W11a decides the served set **first**, as a numbered decision with the user, and
designs only that. Proposed tranches: **sewer 106 settlements** (99.1 % of properties at
12.9–15.4 m/property) · **price 17** (21.1 km, 0.75 % of load) · **do not sewer 31**
(44.7 km, 0.36 % of load, 204 m/property).

### P8 — A lifting station is a commissioning device, not only a depth device

NAMA's packages are chained — `5A-3 → 5A-2 → 5A-4 → 5A-1 → pumping station → 10 km force
main → STP` — so no package can be commissioned before its downstream neighbour exists. The
exception is the lesson: **5A-1 terminates at a pumping station, and that station plus the
only built force main is what let 60.8 km and ~5,963 properties be commissioned without
first building 7 km of deep gravity trunk.**

This inverts how W10 treated stations — as a cost to minimise. They are also the seams that
make the scheme buildable in stages. A package is **3.5–40 km serving 180–2,180 plots, one
connected tree with exactly one outlet**; W10's 206 subnetworks are unusable as packages
(median 1.16 km, largest 265.8 km). W11a carries two fields: `PHASE` and `PACKAGE`.

### P9 — Every constant is re-extracted from its cited page at build time

Two errors are already in the inherited criteria: check A9 caps rising mains at 3.0 m/s
where **G203-p50 says 2.5 m/s** (3.0 is the *gravity* maximum from p27, and the two were
conflated); and A2's material wording disagrees with Table 6 p22. Separately, `INLET_MIN_DEG`
is carried at 85° in the code and 75° in its own docstring and register.

And the whole `p3_optimise` strategy was built against a clause — *"Sewers shall not be
oversized to facilitate flatter slopes"*, G203-p29 — that `TUTORIALS/T02` §6.3 already
documented. Rule: a build-time gate re-reads every cited constant from the PDF page it
claims, and the method is checked against T02 before it is implemented.

### P10 — The as-built is a calibration reference, not a template

It is the best evidence available of how a real engineer solved this problem here, and it is
also wrong in places. Measured: **diameter is a tier label, not a hydraulic response** —
OD160 for every lateral *and* every sub main, one carrying 686 properties, OD200 for the
trunk, and all 20 diameter changes in 63 km are 160→200 at the trunk junction. **110 built
pipes cannot pass today's peak flow.** At a wadi they go **shallower and steeper**, not
deeper. And 37 backdrops exceed 2 m, which under our own G203-p30 rule needs a vortex shaft.

Copy the **shape and the packaging**. Do not copy the sizing.

## The invariants

Always true, asserted continuously, not checked at the end:

1. Every load unit is assigned to exactly one chamber, or listed by name. *(W10 dropped 1,233 m³/d — 1.7 % — silently.)*
2. Reloading the published layers reproduces the design graph exactly.
3. Every pipe passes its own peak flow within its d/D limit. *(2.80 km surcharged.)*
4. Every reach meets minimum cover **using its own outside diameter**. *(45.92 km below, from a hardcoded 0.30 m.)*
5. Depth is checked **between** nodes, not only at them, in every solve that publishes. *(Computed in `p2_sizing` and thrown away.)*
6. No pipe runs along a dual carriageway; every crossing is scheduled. *(1.67 km along, 47 crossings unscheduled.)*
7. No pipe or chamber on wadi ground except a scheduled perpendicular crossing.
8. Every diameter records **what set it** (`SIZED_BY` ∈ capacity / d/D / velocity / horizon). "Depth" is prohibited by G203-p29.
9. Every published number traces to the one function that produced it.
10. No stage silently no-ops. *(`RoadTreatment` ran with `units=None, sampler=None`; three of its steps did nothing and 34 collapsed roundabout rings intersect a registered plot.)*

## Build order

| # | Stage | Gate before moving on |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Auditor** — 59 checks, run against W10's layers | The failing table exists and is the brief |
| 1 | **Scope decision** — the served set, agreed with the user | Numbered decision recorded |
| 2 | **Corridor** — provenance, confidence, wadi and dual rules applied at source, `auto_link` re-cut | Checks D1–D9 pass |
| 3 | **Hierarchy** — tier by the P5 rules, ~270 km of sub main | Checks F1–F5 pass; tier shares near 66/18/5 |
| 4 | **Chambers** — Table 12 spacing, junctions, bends, drops, one outlet | Checks C1–C9 pass |
| 5 | **Hydraulics** — sizing, inverts, cover, stations | Checks A1–A12, B1–B5 pass |
| 6 | **Packages** — `PHASE` and `PACKAGE`, seams at stations | Every package one tree, one outlet, 15–40 km |
| 7 | **Deliverables** — schedules, profiles, SewerGEMS export, drawings | Concept-stage list only; the 16-item do-not-attempt list is honoured |
