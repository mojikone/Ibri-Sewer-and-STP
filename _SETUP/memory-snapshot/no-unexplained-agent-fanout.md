Do the work solo and visibly; do not launch multi-agent workflows (or several background agents) without asking first, even when an "ultracode" mode is on.

**Why:** 2026-10-01, W17 SewerGEMS session — a ~25-agent research workflow (help/KB research plus skeptic verifiers) was launched under ultracode; the engineer asked "why are you running many agents? ... i don't like when i don't understand what's going on."

**How to apply:** default to inline work he can follow; if a fan-out would genuinely help, say what it is, how many agents, and what it touches, and wait for a yes. Related: [[list-tasks-before-multistep-work]], [[plain-wording-not-literary]].
