Never report an item as missing from a report or deliverable without checking the deliverable first; a one-line caption scan settles it. Under a standing "go ahead with applying the changes", don't finish by handing back a list of candidates and asking whether to add them: check each against the deliverable, add what is truly missing, drop what is covered, and report what was done.

**Why:** 2026-11-19, R11. I closed with "four R7 visuals R11 still lacks — add them?" tagged [Likely]. The engineer asked "are you sure they are missing? why you did not apply before finishing the task?" Two of the four (the peak-factor curve, Figure 4-4, and the flood-hazard maps, Figures 1-9 to 1-12) were already in the report, and the other two were covered by tables. The question cost him a round trip and showed the claim was unchecked.

**How to apply:** Before writing "missing", "not yet", or "candidates", grep the built document (captions, headings, tables). If the user has already authorised changes, decide and act. Ask only when the choice is genuinely his: scope, cost or a client-facing judgement. Related: [[no-exemptions-in-compliance-checks]], [[list-tasks-before-multistep-work]].
