Write like a colleague talking, not like a standards document. The user found
heavily-worded, "literature level" phrasing exhausting to read. Keep sentences
short and ordinary. Tables and bullets stay — it's the prose register that was
the problem, not the structure.

**Why:** they read these under time pressure and need to scan; dense formal
prose slows them down and buries the point.

**How to apply:** plain verbs, no rhetorical build-ups, no "it is worth noting
that" or "the important part is". Say the thing. This applies to chat replies
*and* to notes/reports written into the repo. Still keep the advisor stance —
challenge first, confidence tags, page refs — just say it plainly.
