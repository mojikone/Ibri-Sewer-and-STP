Do not write the concept report at R7's level of detail. Edit the team's restructured draft (R8-MH) in place. Keep its chapters, its ideas and its depth, and change only the numbers and any text that contradicts the new basis.

**Why:** the engineer said on 2026-11-16 that the R7 report "was very hard for the team to understand as it hold too much information". The team spent a lot of time reviewing and restructuring it, and changing it back would confuse them and the client.

**How to apply:** before adding a paragraph, table or figure, check whether R8-MH has the same thing at a lighter level. Match that level. Put any extra working in the README or a provenance file, not the report.

**Deliverable for every revision of the team's report (engineer, 2026-11-16): three files.**
- a tracked copy, made in one Word pass with track changes on;
- a clean copy, which is the same file with every change accepted, so the two cannot drift;
- a short change list in the shape of Jaida's `Validation Comments.docx` of 8 Oct: numbers changed, corrections, positions, removed.

The engineer decides which of them to hand over. Both copies go through the clean check.
